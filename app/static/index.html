    Checking your photo

      How a file is checked

      1. Find the faces

        A small face detector (YuNet) finds every face and crops it with some context. Videos are sampled at up to 24 frames.

      2. Two detectors look

        Two public deepfake detectors, a ViT and a SigLIP model, each trained on different kinds of fakes, inspect every face.

      3. Combine the evidence

        A layer trained on 8,075 faces from 13 sources turns both views into one calibrated probability. Videos use a layer trained on video frames.

      4. Explain it

        Grad-CAM shows where the evidence is, and retrieval lists the closest known real and fake faces from the training data.

      Measured on faces it never saw

      Test faces come from people, videos and files kept out of training, plus seven sources it never trained on at all. AUC measures how well fakes rank above real faces: 1.0 is perfect, 0.5 is a coin flip.

      Know the limits

      • A probability, not proof

        Use it alongside other checks, such as where the file came from.

      • Quality matters

        Heavy compression, tiny faces, strong coloured lighting and brand-new generators lower accuracy.

      • Faces only

        A real face placed into a fake scene still reads as real. Talking-head avatars are a known weak spot.
