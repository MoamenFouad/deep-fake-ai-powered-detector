    How a file is checked

    1. 1

      Find the faces

      A small face detector (YuNet) finds every face. Videos are sampled at up to 24 frames.

    2. 2

      Two detectors look

      Two pretrained models, a ViT and a SigLIP, each trained on different kinds of fakes, inspect every face.

    3. 3

      Fuse the evidence

      A fusion layer we trained on four public deepfake datasets turns both views into one probability.

    4. 4

      Explain it

      Grad-CAM shows where the evidence is, and retrieval finds the closest known real and fake faces.

    Measured on faces it never saw

    Test faces come from people and videos kept out of training. AUC measures how well fakes are ranked above real faces: 1.0 is perfect, 0.5 is a coin flip.

    Know the limits

    • A probability, not proof

      Use it alongside other checks, such as where the file came from.

    • Quality matters

      Heavy compression, tiny faces and brand-new generators lower accuracy.

    • Faces only

      A real face placed into a fake scene still reads as real.
